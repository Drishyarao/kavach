How to read this

  • Each image is turned into a small visual "sketch". We count how many parts of the two sketches differ.
  • A low distance means the images look alike. A high distance means they look different.
  • Cropping, resizing, filters, and heavy edits can change the score in either direction.
  • Keep the original links, screenshots, and dates. They matter more than this score.

Safe next steps

  • Keep the original files, and save screenshots, links, and dates before anything is removed.
  • Note the platform and page address where you found the image.
  • Use the platform's own reporting option for the content.
  • Talk to someone you trust. You do not have to handle this alone.
  • Do not confront anyone online or share accusations. It can make things harder.
  • If you feel unsafe right now, contact local emergency services.
