2. We compare the look

3. Get a careful result

A similarity score with a cautious verdict, plus a report you can save.

Please know the limits. This measures visual similarity only. It cannot prove copying, show who shared an image, or detect deepfakes. Heavy edits can fool it either way, so treat the result as a clue, not proof. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services or someone you trust.
